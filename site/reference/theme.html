Reference/API/Theme

Theme

A theme is plain data with public fields. Start from Theme::LIGHT or Theme::DARK and change what the design changes; Themes and motion shows how.

Theme

FieldMeaning
colors: ColorsColour roles.
type_scale: TypeScaleText styles.
spacing: SpacingSpacing scale.
radii: RadiiCorner-radius scale.
fonts: FontsFont families.
MethodWhat it does
light()Same as Theme::LIGHT.
dark()Same as Theme::DARK.
color(token: ThemeColor) -> ColorThe value of a colour token.
font(role: TextRole) -> FontFamilyThe font family of a step of the type scale
text(role: TextRole) -> TextSpecThe text style of a step of the type scale.

Colors

FieldMeaning
background: ColorThe screen background; also the frame's clear colour.
surface: ColorCards, sheets and bars.
surface_variant: ColorInput fields, chips, secondary buttons.
primary: ColorBrand or accent colour.
on_primary: ColorText on primary.
primary_container: ColorA soft tint of primary.
text: ColorMain text.
text_muted: ColorSecondary text.
divider: ColorHairlines and outlines.
danger: ColorDestructive actions and errors.
on_danger: ColorText on danger.
success: ColorPositive amounts and confirmations.
pressed_overlay: ColorPainted over a pressable while a finger is on it (unless the pressable sets its own pressed background).

The default light theme has a cool grey background, white surfaces and a blue accent.

TypeScale

FieldMeaning
display: TextSpecThe one big number or word on a screen (a balance).
title: TextSpecScreen titles.
headline: TextSpecSection and card headings.
body: TextSpecRunning text and list rows.
label: TextSpecButtons, tabs, field labels.
caption: TextSpecMetadata, timestamps, hints.
RoleSizeWeightLine height
display34 dpBold1.2
title24 dpBold1.25
headline17 dpSemibold1.3
body15 dpRegular1.4
label14 dpSemibold1.3
caption12 dpRegular1.35

A step is a TextSpec::new(size, weight, line_height).

Fonts

FieldMeaning
body: FontFamilyRunning text, labels and captions.
heading: FontFamilyDisplay, title and headline text.
MethodWhat it does
new(body: FontFamily, heading: FontFamily)A body family and a heading family.
