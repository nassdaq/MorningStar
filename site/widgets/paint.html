Colours, gradients and shadows

What a Container can be painted with.

Colours

A colour is a value or a role of the theme. Give values as the design writes them.

WriteFor
hex(0x2F6BFF)An opaque colour from #RRGGBB
hexa(0x2F6BFFCC)A colour with alpha from #RRGGBBAA
rgb8(47, 107, 255)An opaque colour from bytes, as CSS rgb(..)
with_alpha(colour, 0.5)The same colour with another opacity
ThemeColor::PrimaryA role, which follows the theme

Colours are composited as sRGB-encoded values, as a browser does, so a translucent colour over a background gives the result a design tool shows.

Gradients

col![
    Container::empty()
        .height(80.0)
        .fill_width()
        .radius(16.0)
        .gradient(Gradient::vertical(hex(0x2F6BFF), hex(0x1B3FA6))),
    Container::empty()
        .height(80.0)
        .fill_width()
        .radius(16.0)
        .background(hex(0x0D0F15))
        // A glow: a circle centred a third of the way down.
        .radial(
            Radial::circle()
                .at(0.5, 0.33)
                .stop(0.0, hexa(0x7C5CFFCC))
                .stop(1.0, hexa(0x7C5CFF00)),
        ),
]
.gap(12.0)
.fill_width()
MethodWhat it does
vertical(top: impl Into<ColorRef>, bottom: impl Into<ColorRef>)From top at the top edge to bottom at the bottom edge.
horizontal(left: impl Into<ColorRef>, right: impl Into<ColorRef>)From left at the left edge to right at the right edge.
diagonal(top_left: impl Into<ColorRef>, bottom_right: impl Into<ColorRef>)From the top-left corner to the bottom-right corner.
angled(degrees: f32, from: impl Into<ColorRef>, to: impl Into<ColorRef>)At an angle with the CSS meaning: 0 runs bottom to top, 90 left to right, 180 top to bottom.
MethodWhat it does
circle()radial-gradient(circle, ..): the same radius in every direction.
ellipse()radial-gradient(ellipse, ..) (CSS's default shape): the box's own proportions.
at(x: f32, y: f32)The centre, as fractions of the box's width and height.
extent(extent: RadialExtent)Where the gradient ends (offset 1).
stop(offset: f32, color: impl Into<ColorRef>)Adds a colour stop at offset (0 at the centre, 1 at the extent).

Several radial gradients go on one box with .radials([..]), in CSS order: the first on top. .blur(sigma) blurs a box's background, which is how a soft glow is drawn. ADR 0033, ADR 0034.

Borders and corners

MethodEffect
.radius(dp)One radius for the background, border, shadow and clip
.radii(tl, tr, br, bl)A radius per corner, for solid and bordered boxes
.border(width, colour)A stroke along the inside of the edge
.border_dashed(width, colour), .border_dash(length, gap)A dashed stroke
.clip()Cuts the children to the box, following its radius

Shadows

.shadow(..) takes one of three ready-made shadows or one built from a design's numbers.

ShadowFor
elevation::LOWA hairline lift: list cards on a tinted background
elevation::MEDIUMA raised card
elevation::HIGHA floating element: hero cards, sheets, floating buttons
Shadow::new(x, y, blur, colour)The numbers a design gives

.shadows([..]) puts several on one box, in CSS order, and takes inner (inset) shadows too. ADR 0035.

Opacity and layers

.opacity(value) draws a widget and everything inside it as one group at that opacity, so overlapping children do not show through each other. ADR 0028.
