SafeArea and Spacer

SafeArea

The screen is drawn edge to edge, under the status bar, the gesture bar and any display cutout. SafeArea keeps its child clear of them.

MethodWhat it does
new<M>(child: impl Into<Element<M>>) -> Container<M>A full-size box that insets child on all four edges.

For a bar that should reach the screen's edge with only its contents kept clear, use .safe_area(edges) on the bar's own box instead:

MethodWhat it does
new(left: bool, top: bool, right: bool, bottom: bool)Edges in the order left, top, right, bottom.

While the keyboard is up, a box that pads for the bottom safe area pads for the keyboard instead.

Spacer

A spacer is an empty box along the parent's axis.

MethodWhat it does
new<M>() -> Container<M>Takes all the space left along the parent's axis, pushing what follows to the far end.
weighted<M>(weight: f32) -> Container<M>Like new, sharing the space left with other fills in proportion to weight.
fixed<M>(dp: f32) -> Container<M>Exactly dp along the parent's axis and nothing across it.

Spacer::new() between two children pushes them to opposite ends; at the end of a column it pushes what follows to the bottom. For a fixed distance between all children, .gap(..) on the parent is simpler.

Pressable

Pressable::new(child) is a box around a child, ready for .on_press(..). It is described with Button.
