Widgets/Overview/Widget gallery

Widget gallery

Every screen is a tree of these widgets. Each page lists the widget's methods, read from the source's own doc comments when the site is built, and shows a sample that is compiled with the workspace.

Layout

ContainerThe box. Padding, a background, a radius, a border, a shadow, and children.Column, Row and WrapChildren one under the other, side by side, or wrapping onto new lines.StackChildren on top of each other, pinned to an anchor.ScrollViewContent taller or wider than its box.SafeArea and SpacerClear of the system's bars, and flexible gaps.

Content

TextText in the theme's type scale, with spans for mixed styles.TextInputAn editable text and its keyboard.Button and PressableFive kinds of button, and anything else that can be tapped.DividerA line between rows.Picture and AvatarImages, SVG files, and people.IconOne-colour vector artwork.LiveVideoA camera or a shared screen from a live room.
