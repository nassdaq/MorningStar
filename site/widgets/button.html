Button and Pressable

Button

A button with a label, in one of five kinds.

col![
    Button::new("Save").on_press(Choice::Save),
    Button::new("Later").secondary().on_press(Choice::Later),
    Button::new("Details").outlined().on_press(Choice::Details),
    Button::new("Skip").plain().on_press(Choice::Skip),
    Button::new("Delete").destructive().on_press(Choice::Delete),
    // No message, no tap: the button is drawn disabled.
    Button::new("Save").on_press_maybe(can_save.then_some(Choice::Save)),
]
.gap(8.0)
MethodWhat it does
new(label: impl Into<Cow<str>>)A filled button in the theme's primary colour.
on_press(message: M)The message a tap delivers.
on_press_maybe(message: Option<M>)Enabled only with Some.
primary()Filled with the primary colour.
secondary()Filled with the quiet SurfaceVariant colour.
outlined()Transparent with an outline.
plain()Label only, in the primary colour.
destructive()For actions that delete or sign out: filled with the danger colour.
radius(dp: f32)Corner radius in dp (12 by default).

A button without a message is drawn disabled and cannot be tapped. .on_press_maybe(..) makes that depend on the state.

A button hugs its label. .fill_width() makes it as wide as its parent, which is what most forms want.

Pressable

Anything can be tapped: .on_press(message) works on every box. Pressable::new(child) is a box around a child, ready for it.

Pressable::new(
    row![
        Avatar::new("Amina Juma").size(40.0),
        col![text("Amina Juma"), text("Order 1042").caption().muted()].fill_width(),
        text("TSh 45,000").label(),
    ]
    .gap(12.0)
    .cross_align(CrossAlign::Center)
    .fill_width(),
)
.padding_xy(16.0, 12.0)
.on_press(order)
.fill_width()
MethodWhat it does
new<M>(child: impl Into<Element<M>>) -> Container<M>A box around child, ready for .on_press(..).
MethodWhat it does
on_press(message: M)Makes the box tappable: a tap on it delivers message to Application::update.
on_press_maybe(message: Option<M>)Like on_press, tappable only with Some.
pressed_background(color: impl Into<ColorRef>)The background while pressed.
block_touches()Swallows touches on the box so that nothing underneath it reacts: a modal scrim or a sheet over other content.

A pressable is one stop for a screen reader and reads the texts inside it in order. One with no text inside, such as an icon button, needs a .label(..). The accessibility check also reports any control smaller than 48 by 48 dp.
